## Challenge Brief

Beacon Innovation Hub (BIH) runs technical projects involving participants
from different technical fields.

BIH has observed that project outcomes vary considerably. Some projects
progress successfully, while others experience delays, low participation,
inconsistent attendance, or weaker review outcomes.

The BIH technical team wants to understand:

> **What factors appear to be associated with participant engagement and
> project performance?**

You have been assigned to investigate the available data and provide
evidence-supported conclusions.

This is an **exploratory and statistical investigation**.

You are expected to decide which analytical methods are appropriate.

---

# Your Role

You are working as a developing Data Scientist.

BIH expects you to:

- Explore unfamiliar data
- Identify data-quality problems
- Ask useful analytical questions
- Select appropriate statistical methods
- Test hypotheses
- Quantify uncertainty
- Distinguish association from causation
- Communicate findings clearly
- Identify what the evidence cannot establish

> You are being assessed on your reasoning, not simply whether your
> Python code runs.
