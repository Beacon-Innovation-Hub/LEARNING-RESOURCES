# Scenario

Mr P.G. Marapira and Mr A.N.N. Sibisi are designing an online shopping
platform that allows customers to browse products, place orders and
purchase products electronically.

As the platform develops, they want the system to do more than simply
process transactions.

They want to investigate whether historical shopping data can be used
to support better business decisions.

The proposed system records information about:

- Customers
- Products
- Product categories
- Shopping sessions
- Orders
- Order items
- Payments
- Discounts
- Product views
- Cart activity
- Delivery information
- Customer reviews

The development team believes Machine Learning could help the platform
understand customer behaviour and improve its operations.

However, no Machine Learning solution has yet been selected.

---

# Your Role

You have joined the project as a developing **Data Scientist**.

Mr Marapira and Mr Sibisi have asked you to investigate the available
e-commerce data and determine:

> **Where can Machine Learning provide useful and defensible decision
> support for the e-commerce platform?**

You are responsible for deciding:

- What should be predicted
- Which data should be used
- Which features are appropriate
- Which models should be tested
- Which evaluation metrics matter
- Whether the models are actually useful

> You are not being assessed on finding the most complicated model.
> You are being assessed on building a valid Machine Learning workflow.
